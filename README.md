## Practicals

| # | Practical | Slides | Step-by-step guide |
|---|-----------|--------|--------------------|
| 1 | InSAR Processing with ESA SNAP: mapping volcano deformation with Sentinel-1, with a real example from Kīlauea, Hawaii, 2018 | [PDF](practicals/01-InSAR-Processing-with-SNAP/InSAR_Processing_with_SNAP_Akshay_Raj_Manocha.pdf) | [SNAP guide](practicals/01-InSAR-Processing-with-SNAP/SNAP_Step_by_Step_Guide.md) |

The step-by-step guide takes students from downloading two Sentinel-1 images of Kīlauea (2 → 8 May 2018) to a finished map of ground movement in SNAP. It gives every menu path and setting, a check after each step, how to compare the result with COMET-LiCSAR, troubleshooting, and the questions to hand in.
