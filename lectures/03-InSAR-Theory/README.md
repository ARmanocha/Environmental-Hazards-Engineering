# Lecture 3: InSAR Theory

**Synthetic Aperture Radar Interferometry** · presented by Akshay Raj Manocha

📄 **Slides:** [InSAR_Theory_Akshay_Raj_Manocha.pdf](InSAR_Theory_Akshay_Raj_Manocha.pdf) (43 slides)
🗒️ **Speaker notes:** [speaker_notes.md](speaker_notes.md)

Follows [Lecture 2](../02-InSAR-Geometry/) and puts its theory to work on real data.

## What the lecture covers

1. **From SAR to InSAR:** SAR amplitude and phase, the electromagnetic spectrum, backscatter (flooding in Louisiana, August 2021), and interferogram formation.
2. **Interferometry for topography:** the interferometric triangle, parallel and perpendicular baselines, the critical baseline (about 5.1 km for Sentinel-1), topographic phase, SRTM and TanDEM-X, and DEM technologies.
3. **Interferometry for surface deformation:** deformation phase, the 1992 Landers earthquake (20 fringes = 56 cm), sensitivity, and phase unwrapping.
4. **Phase contributions and error sources:** orbital ramps, DEM errors, ionospheric artefacts, and tropospheric noise.
5. **Correlation and wavelength:** coherence and its estimation, temporal decorrelation, total correlation, and the 2009 L'Aquila earthquake seen at X-, C- and L-band.

The deck ends with a recap of key numbers, the Geo-SInC course repository, and credits.
